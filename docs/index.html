How it works

  1. Universe: Nifty 200 constituents, filtered for liquidity (₹10 cr+ median daily turnover) and extreme volatility.
  2. Chart reading: about 35 technical features per stock: momentum over 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, relative strength vs Nifty, moving-average trend, 52-week high and low, RSI, MACD, volatility, drawdown, beta and volume accumulation, plus market regime (Nifty trend, breadth, volatility).
  3. Model: gradient-boosted trees trained on 12 years of weekly snapshots. They predict (a) the stock's rank in 5-month excess return, (b) the probability of beating Nifty, and (c) the 10th, 50th and 90th percentile of its 3-, 4- and 5-month return. Training uses only data whose outcome was already known on the prediction date, so there is no look-ahead.
  4. News: recent Google News headlines for each shortlisted company, scored with a finance-tuned sentiment lexicon. When an Anthropic API key is configured, Claude also reads the headlines and flags material events. Stocks with fraud, raid, default or regulator red flags are excluded.
  5. Fundamentals: ROE, growth, margins, leverage and P/E percentile, used as a quality tilt.
  6. Final score = 70% model + 15% news + 15% quality, at most 2 picks per sector. Only the model part is backtested; news and fundamentals aren't available point-in-time historically.
